Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Applies to: the Fabulae Android app (com.fabulae.app), distributed on Google Play.

Who we are: Yordan Creatives, Amstelveen, The Netherlands. Contact: ver.iamyo94@gmail.com. If you are in the EU or UK, this is also the “data controller” for the purposes of the GDPR.

The short version

  • Fabulae does not ask who you are. No name, no email, no account, no sign-in, and no way for us to identify you.
  • Everything you do in the app — your words, your mistakes, your exams, your progress, your chats with the tutor — is stored on your phone only. We cannot read it, because it is never sent to us.
  • The one thing that leaves your phone is the text you type when you use a tutor feature (the chat, a writing correction, an exam answer, a drill). It goes to the AI service that produces the reply, and only at the moment you use that feature.
  • There is no Fabulae server holding your learning data, and there are no trackers, no ads and no analytics in this app.
  • To delete everything, use Settings → Reset all learning data, or clear the app's data, or uninstall. There is nothing of yours on any server to delete.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what the Fabulae app does with your information. It describes version 1.0 of the app, as distributed on Google Play.

If we change how the app handles information, we change this policy at the same time and update the date at the top.

2. In one paragraph

Fabulae is built so that studying never requires an account and never sends your study data anywhere. The only information that leaves your phone is the text you type when you use an AI tutor feature — and only at the moment you use it. We do not operate a server that stores your learning data, and we do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools of any kind.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and the app cannot send us:

  • your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any account credentials (there is no account and no login);
  • your learning data — the words you study, the mistakes you make, your answers, your exam results, your progress, or your transcripts of chats with the tutor;
  • your location, contacts, calendar, photos, files or messages;
  • your device's advertising identifier, IMEI, or any other device or installation identifier;
  • audio or voice recordings (the app has no permission to use your microphone);
  • diagnostics, crash reports or usage analytics. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting software.

The app asks for one Android permission: internet access, so that the tutor features can work.

4. What is stored, and where: on your device only

These live inside the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded to us, and we have no way to read them:

  • your vocabulary cards and their review schedule;
  • every word you have marked as new, learning, known or ignored, and how often you got it right or wrong;
  • your mistakes and the grammar points they belong to;
  • your exam attempts and results;
  • your course progress (which lessons you have finished);
  • your app settings: which languages you study, the voice you chose, your daily practice target and the visual theme;
  • your chat transcripts with the tutor, kept so the conversation continues where you left off.

Not encrypted by us. The app does not put a password or its own encryption on this data; it relies on your phone's own storage protection, which includes full-disk encryption on modern phones. If someone has unlocked access to your phone, they can reach this data the same way they can reach any app's data.

About Android's backup. If you have Android's backup feature switched on, your phone may include Fabulae's data in the backup of your own Google account. That is your phone's behaviour, not something Fabulae asks for, and it is the only circumstance in which Fabulae's data can reach a server. You can turn it off in Android's settings.

If you export a backup file yourself. Settings → Export JSON writes a file and hands it to you. It is your file, kept wherever you choose. We never receive it. Today that file contains your cards, review history, mistakes, grammar, content cache and settings, but not your word statuses, exam results, course progress or chat transcripts. Those three items are also the ones the in-app Reset does not clear (see section 8) — a known gap that is being fixed, and it is listed here rather than described loosely.

5. The only information that leaves your phone

5.1 What is sent, and when

When — and only when — you use one of the four AI tutor features, the app sends the text involved to an AI service to generate the reply:

FeatureWhat is sent
Tutor chat and role-playthe message you typed
Writing correctionthe text you wrote
Exam gradingthe exam question, the marking instructions, and the answer you wrote
Drills for a repeated mistakethe mistake you made and the correct form

Along with your text, the app sends which AI model to use and the technical settings for the request. It also necessarily contains your phone's public internet address, as any request made over the internet does.

Nothing else is sent. No name, no email, no account, no device identifier, no identifier of any kind — because the app does not have any to send.

If you never use the tutor features, nothing leaves your phone at all. The rest of the app — lessons, reading, vocabulary, spaced review and exams — works entirely offline and never contacts the internet.

5.2 Where it goes

Your text is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to:

  • xKiro (https://api.xkiro.com), an AI model routing service, which forwards it to the AI model provider that generates the reply.

xKiro's published privacy policy (last updated 6 July 2026) states that it does not keep the content of requests or responses; that it keeps only request metadata (model, token counts, timestamps, cost, outcome) for billing and operations; and that it may briefly capture content solely to investigate a specific error, deleting it shortly afterwards. It also states that requests are forwarded to the model provider behind the model you are using, and that it keeps technical data (IP address, user-agent, request logs) for security, abuse prevention and debugging.

We do not run this service, we cannot see your requests, and we do not receive a copy of them. The provider's own policy applies to what it does with them, and you can read it at https://xkiro.com/privacy.

5.3 What we never do with your text

We do not read it, we do not store it, we do not use it to train anything, and we do not sell it. We do not receive it in the first place.

6. Reading text aloud

The read-aloud feature uses the text-to-speech engine built into your phone. The app hands the sentence to your device's speech engine (often Google's) — it does not record you, and it does not upload audio. Whether a particular voice is produced on your device or by the speech engine's own service is decided by your device and that vendor, not by Fabulae.

7. Payments

Fabulae is sold on Google Play, with a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the full course. Google processes the payment through its own payment sheet. We never see or receive your card details, your billing address or your name — Google tells the app only that a purchase exists.

8. Your choices

  • Delete your study data: Settings → Reset all learning data (you must confirm twice). This clears your vocabulary cards, review history, mistakes, grammar mastery, study sessions, saved AI content and tutor transcripts. It does not currently clear your per-word statuses, your exam results or your course progress — Android's Clear data for Fabulae and uninstalling both remove everything, including those three.
  • Stop using the AI features: simply do not use them. Everything else keeps working offline. You can also switch off the inline corrections in the tutor chat.
  • Turn off your phone's backup of Fabulae's data: in Android's backup settings.
  • Ask us something: write to ver.iamyo94@gmail.com.

9. Children

Fabulae is a language course for adult learners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children — in fact we collect no personal information from anyone.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Fabulae starts to handle information differently — for example, if the AI features are routed through a Fabulae service instead of going straight to an AI provider, or if optional account backup is added — we will update this policy, change the date at the top, and describe the change in the Google Play release notes for that version.
